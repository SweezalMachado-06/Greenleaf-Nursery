Why plants matter

As more of us live and work indoors, plants are an easy way to bring nature home. Growing and keeping plants offers real benefits:

  1. Beauty & ambience — greenery adds life, colour and calm to any home, office or outdoor space, in every shape and size.
  2. Health & wellbeing — many houseplants help freshen indoor air, and caring for plants can lower stress, lift mood and improve focus.
  3. Sustainability — a home garden encourages a greener, lower-waste lifestyle and supports pollinators and biodiversity.
  4. Homegrown food — vegetable, herb and fruit plants give fresh, chemical-free produce right from your balcony or garden.
  5. Learning — gardening is a hands-on, all-ages way to learn about biology, botany and the natural world.
